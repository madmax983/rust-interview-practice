## 💡 What
Implemented from-scratch foundational systems for Git Object Storage (`src/systems/git_core.rs`) and Tar Archive reading/writing (`src/serialization/tar_archive.rs`).

## 🎯 Why
To provide deep mastery and understanding of how these canonical tools and file formats operate under the hood, adhering to the repository's educational philosophy.

## 📊 Impact
Adds robust, well-documented, and tested implementations of a content-addressable storage system (including SHA-1) and a USTAR archive format parser, demonstrating byte manipulation, serialization, and algorithmic accuracy.

## 🔬 Measurement
`cargo clippy` and `cargo test` pass successfully, ensuring code quality and the correctness of the newly added implementations.
